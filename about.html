About Cuech4

Made by hand. Made on purpose.

Cuech4 started as a way to put original ideas into the world — not mass-produced, not outsourced, just made. Every T-shirt design, sticker sheet, print, and sewn piece starts as a sketch before it becomes something you can hold.

Behind the frog is Edber Elizondo — a creative who believes small-batch work has a kind of honesty that mass retail can't fake. Every order is packed knowing it's going to someone who chose this, not whatever the algorithm pushed.

Materials & craft

What actually goes into each piece

Apparel

  • 100% combed cotton or cotton blend
  • Screen printed, not heat-transfer
  • Pre-shrunk, true-to-size fit

Stickers & prints

  • Weatherproof vinyl for stickers
  • Archival matte paper for prints
  • UV-resistant inks, won't fade

Sewing line

  • Cut and sewn in small batches
  • Natural fibers where possible
  • 3–7 day production, noted per item
Care tip: wash apparel inside-out in cold water and hang dry to protect the print. Handmade items may have small natural variations — that's part of the charm, not a flaw.
